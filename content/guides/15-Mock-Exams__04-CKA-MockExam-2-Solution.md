### How to use the solutions
Compare your approach, not just the final result:
1. Which commands did the solution use? (often a one-line imperative command instead of hand-written YAML)
2. Did it verify the result (`kubectl get`, `describe`)?
3. Which docs page would you open for each unfamiliar field?
Redo each task you missed without looking, and time yourself.

### Tip
Keep a notebook of "slow commands" (e.g. NetworkPolicy YAML, PV/PVC fields, kubeadm upgrade steps) and rehearse them.
