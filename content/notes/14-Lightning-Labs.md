## What this section is about
Timed, mixed-topic labs that imitate the pressure of the exam: several small tasks, each worth a few points, in one cluster.

## How to use them
- Set a timer (the real exam is **2 hours**, about 15-20 tasks, pass mark **66%**) and don't look at solutions until you've tried.
- **Triage**: do quick wins first, flag long tasks (cluster install, upgrades) and return to them. Weights are shown per task.
- After each task verify with a command (`kubectl get`, `curl`, `auth can-i`) before moving on.
- Afterwards, note what slowed you down (YAML syntax? finding a docs page?) and drill that.
